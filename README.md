# muse-officejoke1125i-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [One-Line Office Jokes](https://make.fun/bounty/u-markk-st-one-line-office-jokes-a51f2bcc).

## The entry

> My calendar says focus time, my boss booked a meeting about protecting focus time, and Teams has said 'away' all morning - Teams is the only one telling the truth.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: Three real office details in one line - a calendar focus block, a boss booking a meeting about protecting focus, and a Teams status stuck on 'away' - with the laugh in the last words: Teams is the only one telling the truth.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 9471c27fecb38bab8953c3bbd8292038186f913af79a1c2b400e163b6e8e4632).
